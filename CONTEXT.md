# Minilogue XD Performance

This context describes musical performances and sound control for the Korg Minilogue XD.

## Language

**Score**:
A prepared description of music to perform, including its musical timing and any planned sound changes.
_Avoid_: Patch, program

**Patch**:
A configuration of the synthesizer's sound parameters. A patch is distinct from the music performed with it.
_Avoid_: Score

**Live parameter change**:
A sound-parameter adjustment supplied during score playback. It does not rewrite the prepared score's musical structure.
_Avoid_: Live score editing

**Sound parameter**:
A setting that shapes the XD's sound, including its analog oscillators, digital multi-engine, filter, modulation, and effects.
_Avoid_: Analog parameter when referring to the entire sound-control surface

**Onboard sequence**:
A sequence held and played by the XD's built-in sequencer, distinct from a score performed by a connected computer.
_Avoid_: Score when referring specifically to the built-in sequencer

**Score automation**:
Sound-parameter changes planned at musical times within a prepared score. These are distinct from live parameter changes supplied during playback.
_Avoid_: Live parameter change for a change already contained in the score

**Host clock**:
The computer's timing authority for score playback, which may also drive the XD through outgoing MIDI clock messages.
_Avoid_: External clock when referring to the timing authority of this performance
