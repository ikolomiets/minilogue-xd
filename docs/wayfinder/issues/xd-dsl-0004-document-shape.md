# Choose the structured score document shape

## Question

Which concrete document format and organization should AI/application authors generate, and what makes it reliably validated and versioned?

After the musical semantics are agreed, compare small throwaway examples of the same phrase: notes, a chord, a rest, a tied duration, a tuplet, a tempo change, a patch reference, and timed automation. Include one invalid example and its proposed diagnostic. Discuss pitch spelling, rational values, stable identifiers, references, unknown fields, and compatibility. Decide through human review of the examples; the prototype is an asset, not an implemented parser.
